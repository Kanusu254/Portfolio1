About Me

Hey, I'm Felix The Freelancer, a Digital Marketing Specialist & Software Engineer who helps businesses connect with their audiences and build their online presence through a combination of marketing and technology.

I work across branding, content creation, digital marketing and web development. I enjoy figuring out how to make a business stand out, connect with the right audience and turn that attention into something meaningful.

I believe the best digital experiences happen when marketing and technology support each other. From the way a business attracts its audience to the website or platform where people interact with it, every detail plays a part in shaping how people experience a brand. That's the bigger picture I bring to every project.

My Journey

I started out in digital marketing, exploring how businesses communicate with their customers, build their brands and use digital platforms to reach more people. This sparked my interest in content creation, branding and helping businesses grow their online presence.

As I worked on different projects and my own ventures, I became increasingly curious about the technology behind the websites and digital experiences we use every day. I wanted to go beyond promoting businesses online and understand how to build the digital tools that make their ideas possible.

That curiosity led me to software engineering, where I developed my skills in building websites and web applications. Today, I'm bringing digital marketing and software engineering together to help businesses not only get noticed online but also build the digital experiences that move them forward.

Ideas I am bringing to life

Every project teaches me something different. Here's a look at my own ventures that I've been working on.

01. Urban Haoz

Housing platform

House hunting can be stressful, especially when you're a student trying to find a decent place without wasting time on misleading listings.

Urban Haoz is my attempt to make that process easier through verified rental listings, real property media and a simpler way to connect house hunters with landlords.

My focus: the idea, brand positioning, website structure and digital content.

Status: In development.

02. The Uncharted Tribe

Adventure and experiences

Life is happening out there. The question is, are you actually getting out to experience it?

The Uncharted Tribe is an adventure brand built around getting people outdoors, exploring new places and creating experiences worth talking about long after the trip ends.

I'm exploring its brand identity, content strategy and digital presence while helping turn the bigger idea into something practical.

Status: Brand development and execution.

03. Kuku Mafia

Food business

Good food is one thing. Getting people to notice it, understand your menu and actually place an order is another.

Through Kuku Mafia, I've been exploring how menus, promotional graphics, pricing and customer communication can support a small food business.

It's a chance to learn about branding and marketing where the results matter beyond how good a design looks.

Status: Ongoing business project.
