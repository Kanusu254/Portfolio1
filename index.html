A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I'm Felix. Curious about ideas. Even more curious about making them happen.

DIGITAL MARKETING + SOFTWARE

Ever come across a business online and wonder what made you stop scrolling? I do. I'm curious about the little things that make people notice, click and connect.

That curiosity pulled me into digital marketing. Then I wanted to understand what happens after the click — the websites, tools and technology behind the experience. That's what led me to software development.

Now I enjoy working across both sides: helping ideas find their audience and building digital experiences that bring them to life. I'm always testing, learning and turning ideas into projects.
